# Mexico Node Privacy Model

ChronoOS separates infrastructure intelligence from participant identity.

Public infrastructure data may be represented in the GIS.

Participant nodes are different:

1. Participation requires explicit consent.
2. Residential coordinates are never required.
3. Historical IP addresses are not used to locate participants.
4. Wi-Fi SSID/BSSID information is not stored in the node registry.
5. Node location is represented at city/region precision.
6. Connectivity endpoints may use a relay instead of exposing a residential endpoint.
7. Credentials and private keys are never committed to Git.
