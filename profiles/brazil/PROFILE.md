# ChronoNet Brazil — Advanced Telecom Profile

Country: Brazil
Code: BR
Profile: ADVANCED
Platform: ChronoOS / Chrono Shield Networks

of ChronoNet.

- ANATEL regulatory references
- Spectrum planning
- 5G
- Private networks
- Licensed infrastructure
- Coverage
- GIS
- Media/IPTV
- Chrono Shield infrastructure
- Engineering audit

Data classes are explicitly separated:

PUBLIC
REGULATED
AUTHORIZED
CHRONO_SHIELD_OWNED
CHRONO_SHIELD_MEASURED

A public infrastructure record does not imply operational access
or control of that infrastructure.
